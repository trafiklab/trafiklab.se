---
title: "Trafiklab wins industry award"
date: 2012-03-27

---
Trafiklab has just been awarded the industry's prize for good intentions at the national inspiration day at Berns in Stockholm. The prize was presented by the Minister for Infrastructure, Catharina Elmsäter-Svärd. We are incredibly happy about the award and want to take the opportunity to thank all the creative developers who show that open traffic data is the way forward!
