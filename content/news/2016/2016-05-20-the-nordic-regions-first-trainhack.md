## At the end of August, 30 developers will gather on board a train from Gothenburg to Uppsala when the Uppsala-based startup 46elks, together with Trafiklab and Oxyfi, organises the Nordic region's first hackathon on a train. A special carriage with a fast internet connection and “hack seats” has been chartered so that the hack participants can develop apps or web services that improve the public transport experience for travellers.

![](/media/2016/05/2016-05-20-nordens-forsta-trainhack-1.jpg)

The Trainhack journey takes place on the Blå tåget (Blue Train) between Gothenburg and Uppsala via Stockholm on 25 August. There is room for 30 developers in the chartered carriage. To encourage knowledge sharing and collaboration, the first prize will be awarded to the team judged to be the most helpful.

The purpose of Trainhack is to create a hack event for developers that is unique to Sweden, challenging and fun, and for it to be a journey focused on creating digital services that make public transport easier and a more positive experience throughout Sweden and the EU.

Examples of apps or services that can be developed at Trainhack:

- Create an app/site that finds the best ticket price.
- Create a game about trains or one that is suited to playing on a train.
- Create the most awesome traffic information monitoring/presentation.
- Create an IoT (Internet of Things) solution for traffic information.
- Find cool ways to use GTFS (The General Transit Feed Specification). GTFS defines a common format for public transport, timetables and associated geographic information, which means that developers can write programs that use the data in a compatible way.
- Build an idea entirely of your own!

Trainhack is organised by the Uppsala-based startup 46elks in collaboration with Oxyfi and Trafiklab.

Itinerary for Trainhack 25 August 2016:

- 08:00 We board the train
- 13:37 Arrival in Uppsala
- 14:00 Lunch buffet & more hacking
- 16:00 Presentation and demo of the projects
- 17:00 Mingling
- 18:00 Trainhack ends for this time

For more about Trainhack and how to sign up, see [http://www.trainhack.se](http://www.trainhack.se/)
