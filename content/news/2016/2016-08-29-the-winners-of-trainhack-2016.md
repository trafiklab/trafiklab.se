**The winners of this year's Trainhack have now been chosen! Below is a list of the winning teams, and the link below leads to a video where all the teams present their ideas and concepts.**

![](/media/2016/08/2016-08-29-vinnarna-fran-trainhack-1.jpg)

- **Train Alerts - Jens and Christian (1st prize)**
- **Klimatkollen - Elvira and Lovisa (Trafiklab's prize)**
- **SJ Appar - Tolga (Prize for most innovative)**
- **Låt oss fixa kollektivtrafiken - Victoria (Prize for most collaborative)**
- **Spårinfo - Tomas (Prize for most useful project)**
- **Positionsanalys - Dag (Prize for best data analysis)**
- **XML - CSS - Daniel and Anton (Prize for the hackiest solution)**
- **På Spåret - Jonas and Marcus (Prize for the most entertaining project)**

More information about the competition: [trainhack.se](http://www.trainhack.se)
