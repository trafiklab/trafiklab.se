Soon it's time for a new meetup with Trafiklab. On Thursday 2 June from 4 pm onwards, you are welcome to come and hear what's in the pipeline and what has happened since last time…

On the agenda, among other things:

- Vinnova's work on open data and upcoming calls for proposals in the field
- The Swedish Transport Agency's (Transportstyrelsen) APIs and data sources
- All the information about new versions of the APIs and GTFS from Samtrafiken
- Trafiken.nu/Trafik Stockholm/SLL talk about traveller needs and their ongoing change work

As usual, you'll meet those of us who work with Trafiklab and hang out with API owners from Trafikverket, Samtrafiken and SL.

Date: Thursday 2 June

Time: 4:00 pm

Location: Vinnova, Mäster Samuelsgatan 56

We have a limited number of spots, so don't wait too long to sign up. [Sign up here.](http://goo.gl/forms/gwXSrBo1xkw3tl9c2)

See you there!
