Soon it's time for Trafiklab's meetup again. On Thursday 11 February from 4 pm onwards, you are welcome to come and hear what's in the pipeline and what has happened since last time…

On the agenda, among other things:

- Sveriges Radio will talk about their work with traffic information. And new open content.
- New GTFS feed from Samtrafiken and changes to the ResRobot APIs

As usual, you'll meet those of us who work with Trafiklab and hang out with API owners from Trafikverket and SL.

Date: Thursday 11 February

Time: 4:00 pm

Location: IIS (The Internet Foundation in Sweden), Ringvägen 100A (Skanstull metro station), Stockholm

We have a limited number of spots, so don't wait too long to sign up. [Sign up here.](http://trafiklab.us4.list-manage.com/track/click?u=5a3e91b1ae79e319b8f678731&id=885a090ccf&e=5f5173e775)

See you there!
