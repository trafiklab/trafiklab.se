We want to make it easier to choose to travel sustainably. That's why we believe there is a need for sharper and more fun digital services that help people find their way in public transport. During the autumn, we have worked closely with [UL](https://www.ul.se/) and [Östgötatrafiken](https://www.ostgotatrafiken.se/) to see how we can further sharpen our delivery of public transport data in Sweden. On Monday 29 January in Linköping and Tuesday 30 January in Uppsala, we'd like to meet you to tell you more, and we'll treat you to a wrap and something to drink with it!

UL and Östgötatrafiken are two of the organisations behind [Trafiklab](https://www.trafiklab.se/), where we work together to open up data and APIs from the Swedish public transport sector. We would now like to invite you to two meet-ups if you are interested in developing services based on public transport data or are curious about what others have done.

At these events, you will hear more about the data we make available through Trafiklab and how others have developed services based on public transport data, and you'll get to meet those of us who work at UL, Östgötatrafiken and Trafiklab. We also want to present a couple of exciting new data sources for these regions…

*Trafiklab is a community for open traffic data. A place where you as a developer can access data and APIs for public transport in Sweden and easily get the information you need to develop sharp services. We also want to make it easier for you to show what you've built and to get inspiration from what others have already created. Östgötatrafiken and UL develop and operate public transport in the counties of Östergötland and Uppsala respectively, and are two of the organisations behind Trafiklab.*
