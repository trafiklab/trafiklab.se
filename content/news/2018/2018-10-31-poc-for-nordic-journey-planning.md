Entur in Norway has developed a POC (Proof of Concept) for a journey planner using available GTFS data from Sweden, Norway, Finland and Denmark. The POC is built on the new version of Open Trip Planner, which is still under development.

(Unfortunately the Finland ferries are missing, as you can see in the picture :))
