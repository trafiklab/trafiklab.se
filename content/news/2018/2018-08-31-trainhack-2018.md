It's finally time for this year's edition of TrainHack. This year, Max and Martin will take us on a journey from Stockholm to Duved/Åre and then back to Stockholm.

Trainhack is an event where a group of creative people go on an unforgettable journey through Sweden with the goal of making public transport better.
