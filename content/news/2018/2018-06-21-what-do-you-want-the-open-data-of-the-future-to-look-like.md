This year we will be in Almedalen. Together with the Government Offices of Sweden (Regeringskansliet) and Samtrafiken, Trafiklab will hold an event about open traffic data on the afternoon of Sunday 1 July.

The theme is: How is public transport travel affected by digitalisation and the development of open data? Don't miss this chance to influence politicians and the business community!
