On 10 April we are once again holding a Trafiklab Meetup and would love for you to take part! This time we'll be at the public transport industry's new modern meeting place, Kollektivtrafikens hus.

We kick off at 17:15 and will go on for 1-2 hours.

During the evening we want you and the other Trafiklab users to join in discussing the future of Trafiklab. What's good? What can we develop? How do we make things easier for you? In short, share your ideas, big and small.

As usual we'll offer some beer and something simple to eat, and you'll get the chance to mingle with the other participants.

On the agenda, among other things; SL, Trafikverket and Samtrafiken will talk about what's happening with their APIs. In addition, an exciting inspirational speaker will be joining us!

For more information about the event visit our [blog]({{< relref "/news" >}}) or follow us on [twitter](https://twitter.com/trafiklab).

**Date:** 10 April

**Time:** 17:15

**Location:** Kollektivtrafikens hus, right next to Stockholm Central Station. Centralplan 3, 111 20 Stockholm.

First come, first served, the number of places is limited. Email [linnea.bjorkman@samtrafiken.se](mailto:linnea.bjorkman@samtrafiken.se) to sign up.

See you there?
