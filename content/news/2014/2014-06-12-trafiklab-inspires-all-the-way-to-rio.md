**Today the football World Cup starts in Rio de Janeiro, and in 2016 the Olympic Games will be held in the same city. That is why the city needs to quickly expand and develop both its infrastructure and its IT and mobile communications. One of Trafiklab's founders, the research institute Viktoria ICT, has produced a pre-study on traffic information – Time Rio – to get the city of Rio to build an equivalent of Trafiklab, writes the magazine Ny Teknik.**

It was IT consultant José Luiz Barbosa who, 2 years ago, started a consulting company to help solve some of Rio de Janeiro's challenges.

Rio's mayor has the goal of trying to get people to use public transport more and to get cars off the streets. But there is a lack of accurate real-time information about when the buses, ferries and metro run or don't run.

José Luiz Barbosa brought the problem with him to Sweden and did some research. Here he was inspired by apps such as ”Res i Stockholm” and Skånetrafiken's app. As a result, he contacted Elias Arnestrand, service manager for Trafiklab at Samtrafiken i Sverige AB, to work on precisely that: travel service solutions built on open traffic data.

Viktoria Swedish ICT, one of Trafiklab's founders, with senior researchers Martin Gustavsson and Daniel Rudmark, took an interest in the city's problems and produced a pre-study on traffic information – Time Rio – to get the city to build a platform equivalent to Trafiklab.

The pre-study is now complete, and a larger collaboration project with Swedish companies and Brazilian partners is being planned.

– We at Trafiklab are of course curious about the development of Brazil's open data platform and proud to be an inspiring operation, says Elias Arnestrand.
