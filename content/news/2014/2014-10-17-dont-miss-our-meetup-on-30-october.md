It's time for a meetup with Trafiklab! We want to meet to tell you a bit about what's going on, and get input on what we do and what could be better. We also have some really interesting guests who will talk about, among other things, how to work with GTFS and the national cycle network from NVDB.

From the programme:

- **Bike + public transport = true?** National cycle network through the National Road Database (NVDB).
- Swedish programming champion **Mårten Wiman** talks about the Reskollen app and how he implemented an offline mode using the GTFS files from Trafiklab.
- **Finally!** Presentation of SL's new journey planner API.
- **Open Space** - feel free to suggest topics in the invitation.

As usual we will offer something simple to eat and drink. This time we have a limited number of places, so sign up as soon as possible - first come, first served.

**Date:** Thursday 30 October

**Time:** 17:15

**Location:** Kollektivtrafikens Hus, Centralplan 3, Stockholm (the same building as Stockholm Central Station)
