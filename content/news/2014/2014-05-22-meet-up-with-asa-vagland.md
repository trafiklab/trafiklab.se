The Government has taken note of today's problems with getting information about, and buying tickets for, public transport journeys. It has appointed an inquiry into information, booking and ticket sales for public transport journeys. One question in the inquiry is whether transport companies should be required to provide data that enables services making things easier for travellers in these areas. You can find more information about the inquiry here: [http://www.regeringen.se/sb/a/239752](http://www.regeringen.se/sb/a/239752)

The Government's inquiry chair Åsa Vagland is interested in meeting people with ideas about this, also from outside the established transport actors. We are now looking for thoughts and input on how this area can be developed, and for people with experience of developing services using open data. That is why Samtrafiken, together with Vinnova, would like to invite you, if you are interested in these questions, to a meeting. Åsa will present her assignment and there will be opportunities for input and discussion.

**Time:** Monday 16 June, 16:00 - approx. 18:00

**Location:** Stockholm, Kollektivtrafikens hus, Centralplan 3 (southern part of Stockholm Central Station)

**Registration:** [Here](https://docs.google.com/forms/d/15QYZqn9qBR69OkaIherIdEPJx_kJ_KisMmkeMRlmw84/viewform)

**Contact:** [linnea.bjorkman@samtrafiken.se](mailto:linnea.bjorkman@samtrafiken.se) 072-213 00 33

We offer simple sandwiches and drinks; please state any dietary requirements in the registration.

If you are unable to attend, we have also created a Hackpad where you can leave input for the inquiry. The Hackpad is open and you can find it here: [https://trafiklab.hackpad.com/](https://trafiklab.hackpad.com/)
