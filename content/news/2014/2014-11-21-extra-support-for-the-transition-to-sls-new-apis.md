---
title: "Extra support for the transition to SL's new APIs"
date: 2014-11-21

---
As we have previously announced, SL has released new APIs on Trafiklab. The new APIs will provide improved operational stability and are prepared for upcoming features, such as real-time information in the journey planner. This also means that the old APIs will stop working.

We are now offering extra support for those of you who still have services using the old APIs and need to switch to the new ones. Between 09:00 and 17:00, from 24 November up to and including 28 November, we will have extra staff with expert knowledge of the new APIs available for questions and answers. The hope is that you will be able to get answers to your questions really quickly. Make sure to take advantage of this opportunity! Ask questions or read other people's questions and answers here: [https://kundo.se/org/trafiklabse/](https://kundo.se/org/trafiklabse/)

The old APIs will stop working on 28 February 2015.
