Yesterday's meetup had over 70 registered participants and the evening was a success, to say the least. A new item on the agenda this time was ”open space”, which meant that participants had the opportunity to bring up any topic they liked. During this item, the company Fidesmo, among others, presented new ideas about travel cards in public transport and buying tickets on your mobile.

The evening offered plenty of interesting discussions; among other things, we talked about the benefits of implementing an offline mode using GTFS files, not least for tourists who don't want to use data roaming.

As usual, the evening ended with mingling, where participants got the chance to exchange experiences and ask further questions to the API owners.
