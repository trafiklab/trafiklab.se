Over 65 visitors came to the new Kollektivtrafikens Hus to talk about open data/open APIs. Among other things, they heard about Play and Akka, ResLedaren - the winner of Travelhack 2013, as well as updates from Trafiklab, SL and Trafikverket. The last part gave very good input to the API owners, and there were many questions from the developers.

_“A meetup is a good way to understand the needs of the users who develop the traveller services of the future based on open data”_, Lars-Olof Hjärp, Trafikverket
