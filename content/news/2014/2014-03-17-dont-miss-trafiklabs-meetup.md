It's time again!

We want to meet, so on 10 April we have organised a meetup for those of you who want to help develop the public transport of the future.

The evening's programme looks as follows:

- Going Reactive - How Play and Akka help you build reactive, scalable and fault-tolerant applications and services in Java and Scala (Typesafe)
- ReseLedaren - What happened next?
- Coming up at Trafiklab - SL, Trafikverket, Samtrafiken

We end the evening with mingling and offer simple food and drinks!

**Date**: 10 April

**Time**: 17:15

**Location**: Kollektivtrafikens hus, right next to Stockholm Central Station. Centralplan 3, 111 20 Stockholm.

We still have places left! Sign up (and feel free to bring a friend) today at [linnea.bjorkman@samtrafiken.se](mailto:linnea.bjorkman@samtrafiken.se)

See you there?
