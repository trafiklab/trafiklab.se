**On 5-7 September 2014, East Sweden Hack takes place, with data supplied by Trafiklab. The task is to create apps and web services that benefit residents and visitors in the East Sweden region. The top prize is a trip including accommodation and a study visit to Silicon Valley.**

East Sweden Hack was held for the first time in 2013. Back then the top prize was a trip to one of the world's leading web conferences in Paris. This year the winning team gets to travel all the way to Silicon Valley and the opportunity to make contacts for the future. In addition, the best entry in the ”health” category wins SEK 20,000. The hack is open to programmers, graphic designers, project managers and marketing talents. Each team should consist of 3-4 people, but you can also sign up individually and get help finding teammates.

Read more and sign up at:
