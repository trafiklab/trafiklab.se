The competition is about building the smartest and best service so that those who drive goods find out about disruptions before they get stuck. The competition is organised jointly by the City of Gothenburg, Trafikverket and Viktoria Swedish ICT in collaboration with several goods carriers.

You can find more information about times, prizes and the challenge at [http://gdc2014.se/](http://gdc2014.se/)
