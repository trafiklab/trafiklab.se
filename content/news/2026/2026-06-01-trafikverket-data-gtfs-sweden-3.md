---
title: "Trafikverket data for all rail traffic now available in GTFS Sweden 3"
date: "2026-06-01"
image: '/media/2024/09/banner_sj_oresundsbro.jpg'
---

Trafikverket data for all rail traffic in Sweden is now available in GTFS Sweden 3. This means that all passenger train
traffic is now included in our aggregated feed.

As Trafikverket is the single source of truth for rail traffic in Sweden, some regional train lines that were previously
included in GTFS Sweden 3 have now been replaced with Trafikverket data. As a result, some trains may temporarily lose
realtime data in our aggregated feed. All realtime data remains available in our GTFS Regional feeds.

In the future, we aim to provide realtime data for all trains included in Trafikverket’s data. This will restore
realtime data availability for the affected trains while also extending realtime coverage to trains across Sweden.

The full documentation for our GTFS Sweden 3 API is
available [on Trafiklab](https://www.trafiklab.se/api/gtfs-datasets/gtfs-sweden/). If you have any questions, please
reach out to us [through our support forum](https://support.trafiklab.se).
