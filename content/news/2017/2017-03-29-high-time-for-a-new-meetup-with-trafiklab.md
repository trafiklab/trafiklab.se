On Tuesday 25 April from 17:00 onwards you are welcome to hear what is going on and what has happened since last time…

This time we will be at Taxi Stockholm, who will talk about their API and future plans. A new API will premiere (if everything goes according to plan), and there will be information about a couple of incredibly exciting upcoming events. As usual you will also meet those of us who work with Trafiklab and hang out with API owners from Trafikverket, Samtrafiken and SL.

Date: Tuesday 25 April

Time: 17:15

Place: Taxi Stockholm, Luntmakargatan 64 (metro station Rådmansgatan)

We have a limited number of places, so sign up as soon as you can. Unfortunately, the places usually run out.

Sign up [here through meetup.com](http://meetu.ps/38l87v). Contact [elias.arnestrand@samtrafiken.se](mailto:elias.arnestrand@samtrafiken.se) if you have something you would like to present or think should be on the agenda.

See you there!
