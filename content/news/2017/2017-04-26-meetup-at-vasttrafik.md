**On Tuesday 16 May, Västtrafik is hosting a meetup in Gothenburg. Sign up here: [https://www.meetup.com/Vasttrafik-Utvecklarportalen/events/239480359/](https://www.meetup.com/Vasttrafik-Utvecklarportalen/events/239480359/)**

Welcome to a meetup with Västtrafik's developer portal in collaboration with Triplet and Trainhack!

On Tuesday 16 May from 17:15 onwards you are welcome to hear what is going on with our APIs. Trainhack will give a short talk about what is planned for this year's event. The developers behind Triplet will also come and talk about their experiences and ideas.
