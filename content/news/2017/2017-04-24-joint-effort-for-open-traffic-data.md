**During autumn 2016, a number of stakeholders came together in an effort to develop a target vision for open traffic data in Sweden. The work has now been compiled in a final report with an adopted target vision for continued work in this area.**

You can find the final report [here](https://samtrafiken.se/wp-content/uploads/2017/04/Slutrapport-_-Kraftsamling-%C3%96ppna-Trafikdata-en-m%C3%A5lbild-f%C3%B6r-Sverige-v-1.0-_-Diarienummer-Vinnova-2016-03467.pdf).
