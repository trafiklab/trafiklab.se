**20 teams of young innovators gathered in Vallasstaden in Linköping to compete in developing solutions for the cities of the future.**

**Trafiklab was there together with Östgötatrafiken.**

During the weekend of 8-10 September, the 5th edition of East Sweden Hack was held in Vallastaden in Linköping. Vallastaden is a brand-new district, planned and built with new thinking on sustainable urban development. Until 24 September, the houses and the area are open to visitors who want to see what the cities of the future might look like.

In the midst of these colourful house facades and daring architecture, 20 teams met to compete in developing new innovative solutions for the cities of the future and their residents.

Östgötatrafiken is a partner in organising the event and makes its data available through Trafiklab. Östgötatrafiken had its own representative on site, and Samtrafiken contributed a representative for Trafiklab for technical support and assistance.

Among the teams there is a wide range of different innovations. Everything from solutions for calculating solar panels for your house, a computerised medicine jar for people with dementia and an app for getting in touch with new fika friends at a large workplace. Another team developed measuring equipment for waste bins so that waste management can be better optimised.

Last year's winners of ESH were back and made a chatbot for Facebook Messenger, called ELVA: eLinköpings Virtual Assistant. A virtual assistant you can write to and ask questions, and it answers as well as it can.

By combining different data sources you can get answers about weather, events and public transport journeys, etc., and all without having to install a new app on your phone.

A presentation of all the entries and who won can be found at [eastswedenhack.se](http://eastswedenhack.se/).
