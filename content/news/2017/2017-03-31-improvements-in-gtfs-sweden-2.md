For a long time there have been shortcomings in the naming of certain transport modes in the Route_long_name field in the GTFS export on Trafiklab. Since 30 March, the GTFS files produced have been improved in two ways:

- Previously, SL's local railways were named commuter train in the export. This has now been fixed so that the following names are used: Roslagsbanan [line number], Saltsjöbanan [line number], Lidingöbanan [line number], Tvärbanan [line number] and Nockebybanan [line number].
- Previously, Östgötapendeln was named commuter train in the export. This has now been fixed so that the name Östgötapendeln is used.

[Feel free to get in touch](https://kundo.se/org/trafiklabse/) if you have any questions or thoughts about these improvements.
