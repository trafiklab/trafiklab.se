Trafiklab is establishing a Hello World! scholarship with the ambition of fostering children's interest in digital creativity.

The scholarship recipient will be selected before Christmas and will get the opportunity in 2018 to take part in a camp week with Hello World! and to attend the 2018 Meetups free of charge. Applications are open until 10 December, apply now!

[https://helloworld.se/stipendieansokan/](https://helloworld.se/stipendieansokan/)
