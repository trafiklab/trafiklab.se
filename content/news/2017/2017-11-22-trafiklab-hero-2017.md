**The Trafiklab Hero of the Year 2017 award goes to Thomas Tydal. Thomas is something of a legend in the railway sector, as his solutions have made his colleagues' everyday work easier for a decade and a half.**

The jury chose the winner with the following motivation:

*Through dedicated work over a long period, Thomas Tydal has contributed to the digitalisation of the Swedish railway system. With one foot in the train driver profession and the other in programming, Thomas Tydal has created new, creative and data-driven solutions to challenges for those working on board trains in Sweden. Thomas Tydal's tools help train drivers and on-board staff get good digital support in their work every day, and they also enable staff on Swedish trains to better inform passengers on board and help those managing depots and stabling sidings minimise the risk of disruptions in the railway system. Through his work, Thomas has challenged the industry's stakeholders and driven development forward with constructive proposals. Thomas's work shows the power of a data-driven approach to tackling major societal challenges.*

The award ceremony takes place at Nordic Open Data Forum in Stockholm on 20 November 2017.

**Solutions for the industry and the public**

Since the start, Thomas has focused on developing solutions aimed at staff in various parts of the railway sector, but going forward he will also work on solutions aimed at the general public.

After several years of a growing business, Thomas has founded the company Railit. The company offers the railway's stakeholders the platform and coordination tools needed for punctuality, which leads to increased competitiveness for the railway.

Read more about the company Railit at [www.railit.se](http://www.railit.se/).

**About the Trafiklab Hero award**

[Trafiklab Hero of the Year](http://www.hjalte.trafiklab.se/) was established in 2015 and is awarded to people who have contributed to the development of digital services in public transport built on open data or open APIs from Trafiklab. This year's jury consists of Carin Carelind (Östgötatrafiken), Daniel Rudmark (Viktoria Swedish ICT), Sara Rhudin (Swedish Association of Local Authorities and Regions), Rickard Dahlstrand (Internetstiftelsen), Lars-Olof Hjärp (Trafikverket) and Johan Berg (Trafiklab Hero of the Year 2015). The award is backed by Trafiklab, a community for open traffic data run by Samtrafiken, the public transport industry's joint collaboration company
