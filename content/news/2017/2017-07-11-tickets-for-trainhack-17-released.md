It is now confirmed that Trainhack 2017 will run 7-9 September on what is probably the world's longest route for a rolling hack. Tickets have now been released - book a place today if you want to be sure to get in.

Trafiklab is of course a sponsor again this year. Below is more information from the team behind Trainhack.

![](/media/2017/07/2017-07-11-biljetter-till-trainhack-17-slappta-1.jpg)

*Photo from Travelhack 2016.*

We start at 18:00 in Gothenburg and finish in Uppsala at lunchtime on the 9th.

The route will go via Luleå, where we make a stop for activities and food.

Tickets for Trainhack 2017 are now available at [https://www.trainhack.se](https://www.trainhack.se/)!

Every participant gets their own bed to sleep in, so you can sleep through the whole event if you want to. Hopefully some will manage to stay awake for a while and work on their projects too.

Since we will be on board night trains a lot during this year's event, many of the event's themes will suit night trains. Prizes and themes are not finalised yet but here is a taste:

- **Offline** Since night trains often don't have very good reception, it's good to make things that also work offline; we are curious about the best offline solution.
- **Virtual Reality** Reality on a train can be hard to get perfect, so why not have a private cinema on board while you are lying in your bed, for example. SJ will renovate its night trains over the coming years; do you want to show how awesome it could be?
- **Internet of Things** On board today's trains there is very little passenger-facing technology, so why not put an RFID tag on every seat and let the passengers check in there with their phone?
- **Planning support** Today it takes a long time for train companies to adapt when something happens on the railway; there is no system that can automatically replan timetables based on the current situation. Maybe it's time to create a train simulator that simulates the entire Swedish railway network, so that the consequences of a new timetable are known immediately?
- **Entertainment** How can you make time pass quickly when travelling? During Trainhack 2016 a game was created that asked questions based on where you are, using the GPS position, to give some information about where you are in the world.
- **Good sleep** What can technology do to make it easier to sleep on night trains? Maybe noise-cancelling headphones, whale music or an eye mask are the thing, or is it the fear of not waking up when you need to get off that is disturbing?

There will be WiFi on board all the way; a local server for git repos etc. will also be available.

All participants will be invited to a Slack, where those who don't already have a team can find teammates.
