In August 2016 the 31st Summer Olympic Games begin in Rio de Janeiro, Brazil. During the Games, the region's 12 million inhabitants will be joined by roughly another 500,000 visitors. This will place heavy demands both on the city's transport system, in the form of e.g. buses, metro and boats, and on innovative ways of presenting how to get around the city, e.g. between hotels and arenas.

To bring forward more services, the [Olympic City Transport Challenge](http://trafiklab.us4.list-manage.com/track/click?u=5a3e91b1ae79e319b8f678731&id=48601de384&e=5f5173e775) competition is now starting

The competition has two phases: first a concept phase in which competing teams submit proposals for services that could meet the transport challenges in Rio de Janeiro (the deadline is 7 December 2015). After that the submitted entries are assessed, and those the jury judges to be the best go on to an implementation phase running between 23 December 2015 and 15 March 2016). The jury then makes a final assessment of the entries and a winner is chosen.

The competition has three categories: Travel planner, Comfort and accessibility, and Experience the Olympics, and all winners will be launched under the city's brand Cidade Olímpica. The winners in Comfort and accessibility and Experience the Olympics will also receive a trip to Rio de Janeiro worth up to SEK 25,000.  The competition is organised by Viktoria Swedish ICT in collaboration with Rio de Janeiro and Evry AB.
