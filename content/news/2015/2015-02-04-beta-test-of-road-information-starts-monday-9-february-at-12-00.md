**Trafikverket's open API is now being extended with road-related traffic information. Join as a beta tester and try out the new datasets!**

During a test period we welcome old and new users to help us test our open API with road-related information. The plan is to publish the production version of the road information during the first quarter of 2015.

We want to collect plenty of feedback from everyone who tests. It can be about experiences, impressions or opinions on the data model. We look forward to lots of feedback – the goal is to be able to offer the best possible service.

Send your feedback to: [trafikinfotjanster@trafikverket.se](mailto:trafikinfotjanster@trafikverket.se)

**The new information set includes:**

- **Icon** - Icons, for example for use in graphical user interfaces and maps.
- **RoadCondition** – Road conditions
- **RoadConditionOverview** – Road condition overviews
- **Situation** - Situations containing incidents and disruptions, for example: Important information, Accidents, Reduced load-bearing capacity, Events, Deviating ferry times, Convoy driving, Queue warnings, Unforeseen obstacles, Roadworks.
- **WeatherStation** - Weather stations with measurement data
