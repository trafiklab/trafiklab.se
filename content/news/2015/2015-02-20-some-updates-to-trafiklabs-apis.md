---
title: "Some updates to Trafiklab's APIs"
date: 2015-02-20
draft: true

---

Trafiklab's old APIs TrainExport and TrainInfo have remained on the underlying backend systems. On 15 March they will be removed for good, which means that your keys and API calls will stop working against these APIs.

The super version of the Resrobot Sök Resa API, which contains real-time information from Västtrafik and Skånetrafiken, will be removed from Trafiklab on 15 March 2015 due to poor data quality and low usage.
