It's almost time for Trafiklab's meetup! On Wednesday we'll get together to spend an evening at Openlab in connection with [Commutathon]({{< relref "/news/2015/2015-05-22-commutathon-collaboration-with-sr-openlab-and-other-transport-partners.md" >}}).

On the agenda, among other things:

- Ellen Sundh: The world of sensors! How can we combine collected data with physical machines and discover the unpredictable in the APIs and data around us?
- Carpooling API from Skjutsgruppen.
- Open TTD - minecraft for Swedish public transport.

As usual you'll meet those of us who work with Trafiklab and hang out with API owners from Trafikverket and SL.

**Date:** Wednesday 10 June.

**Time:** 17:00.

**Location:** OpenLab's premises at Valhallavägen 79 in Stockholm.

We have a limited number of places, so don't wait too long to sign up. [Sign up here!](https://docs.google.com/forms/d/1ooEYW-ZVXsmqTid8LshspY307MiVH5-sz_4Hh4xqaxs/viewform?c=0&w=1)

See you there!
