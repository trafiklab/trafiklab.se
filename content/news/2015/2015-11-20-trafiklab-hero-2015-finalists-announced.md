**The finalists in Trafiklab Hero 2015 have been selected. In total, five finalists are competing for the honour as well as money to be spent on a study trip. The winner will be presented on 24 November during Internet Discovery Days in Stockholm.**

– With the competition we want to highlight volunteer enthusiasts, so it's especially nice to see so many qualified nominations and finalists, says Elias Arnestrand, service manager for Trafiklab.

Trafiklab Hero aims to highlight and celebrate all the enthusiasts who contribute to easier, smoother and more fun ways of travelling by public transport in Sweden. Nominations were made via hjalte.trafiklab.se, where anyone could nominate themselves or someone they consider worthy of becoming Trafiklab Hero 2015. The winner receives SEK 20,000 to be spent on a study trip together with a partner of the winner's choice.

– The jury's discussion was intense and the decision was difficult, with several nominations meeting the criteria. The genuine interest in open data and in users' needs was decisive, says jury member Pernilla Rydberg, head of ISS Internetfond.

The Trafiklab Hero 2015 finalists:

**Martin Thuresson**

[Martin Thuresson](http://www.yathra.se/) has taken on the enormous challenge of opening up fare information from public transport operators as an open API. The API enables other developers to create services with fare information even though this information has not yet been released as an open API.

**Johan Nilsson**

[Johan Nilsson](https://www.linkedin.com/in/johanjohan) created the app [STHLM Traveling](http://markupartist.com/sthlmtraveling/) for himself six years ago so that he could get around as easily as possible and not feel lost in Stockholm's public transport. Today the app has grown to become one of the most popular apps in its category.

**Maja Söderberg**

Maja Söderberg is one of the initiators of [Mobilsamåkning](http://www.mobilsamakning.se/), a carpooling service that complements and strengthens public transport in rural areas. Mobilsamåkning is available in 13 Swedish villages and is expected to expand to the Netherlands as well.

**Joel Holmberg**

Together with Hampus Sethfors, Joel Holmberg has built the app [ResLedaren](http://www.resledaren.se/), which supports people with cognitive and/or mental disabilities and/or visual impairments in travelling independently on public transport throughout Sweden.

**Theodor Storm**

Theodor Storm is the creator of [Tågtavlan](http://www.tstorm.se/), one of Sweden's largest operator-independent apps for train information, with around 150,000 users. A Linux server with custom-built software in PHP/MySQL continuously retrieves current train traffic information from Trafikverket's open API and handles about 100,000 API calls from Tågtavlan's users every day.
