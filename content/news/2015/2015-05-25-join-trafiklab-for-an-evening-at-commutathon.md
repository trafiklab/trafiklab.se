Welcome to a meetup with Trafiklab at Commutathon, a hackathon aimed at developing concepts that make daily commuting easier for Stockholmers with mobile services. The hackathon is organised by Sveriges Radio together with OpenLab, Trafik Stockholm, the City of Stockholm and Trafiklab. [You can read more about the hack here!]({{< relref "/news/2015/2015-05-22-commutathon-collaboration-with-sr-openlab-and-other-transport-partners.md" >}}) The hack will be held at OpenLab on 10 – 12 June and Trafiklab will hold a meetup on 10 June.

We have a seriously good agenda – more about it soon on this page.

(psst! Among other things, Ellen Sundh will invite us into the world of sensors and tell us how we can combine collected data with physical machines and discover the unpredictable in the APIs and data around us).

During the evening you'll meet other developers and API owners who are keen to hear what you've done with data from Trafiklab. We'll provide some light food and drinks during the evening.

Have you developed something awesome based on Trafiklab's data that you'd like to show or talk about? Send an email to [linnea.bjorkman@samtrafiken.se](mailto:linnea.bjorkman@samtrafiken.se) and tell us!

**Date:** 10 June

**Time:** 17:00

**Location:** OpenLab's premises at Valhallavägen 79 in Stockholm.

Limited number of places. First come, first served! [Sign up here!](http://goo.gl/forms/Du3OXeI9Lc)

See you there!
