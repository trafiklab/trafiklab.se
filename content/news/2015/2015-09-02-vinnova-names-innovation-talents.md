date: 2015-09-02
draft: true

---

Sweden's innovation agency VINNOVA is recognising today's innovators through the new award Innovationstalangerna. The initiative is carried out together with Veckans Affärer. The purpose of the award is to highlight Sweden as an innovation nation and update the image of today's innovator.
