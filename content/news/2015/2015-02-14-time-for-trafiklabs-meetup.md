On 12 March we're once again holding a Trafiklab Meetup. This time we'll be at .SE's offices on Ringvägen in Stockholm.

**We kick off at 16:30 and carry on for 1-2 hours.**

On the agenda, among other things;

- Trafiklab overview – What is Trafiklab and which APIs are available?
- .SE (Stiftelsen för internetinfrastruktur) talks about new top-level domains
- SL talks about new features in the journey planner API
- Trafikverket – New road data in Trafikverket's API
- Trafiklab's users - Some projects built on Trafiklab's data are presented by their creators

**Date:** 12 March

**Time:** 16:30

**Location:** .SE, (Stiftelsen för internetinfrastruktur), Ringvägen 100, 103 91 Stockholm

All places are now taken, but you can put yourself on the waiting list! Email [linnea.bjorkman@samtrafiken.se](mailto:linnea.bjorkman@samtrafiken.se)

During the evening you'll meet other developers who have created awesome projects, and API owners who are keen to hear what you've done with data from Trafiklab.

As usual we'll provide drinks and some light food, and you'll get the chance to mingle with the other participants.
