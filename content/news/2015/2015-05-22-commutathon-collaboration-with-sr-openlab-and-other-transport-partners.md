**How can we make daily commuting easier for Stockholmers with the help of their mobile phones? That's what Sveriges Radio wants to explore together with Openlab, Trafik Stockholm, the City of Stockholm and Trafiklab - in the Commutathon hack!**

Sweden's capital is growing at a rapid pace. In recent years Stockholm has grown by almost 40,000 people per year. That corresponds to an annual population growth of about 2 percent. This trend is expected to continue and in 2022 the county is forecast to have a population of about 2.5 million.

Every day hundreds of thousands of people commute to and from work, school and other activities. As the city becomes denser, commuting distances get longer and travellers become more numerous, this puts greater pressure on the infrastructure.

The commuting situation thus becomes vulnerable.

Today there is a range of services for those who want help with their commute. For example, there are services that describe the traffic situation for motorists on the major roads, services that deliver video streams of congestion, disruption information for public transport, weather information services, information about roadworks and closures, and of course the important traffic reports on the radio.

All these services exist today. But nowhere are these services gathered together and accessible in a way that is easy for the traveller. Especially not on the mobile phone.

**Through a hackathon called “Commutathon” on 10 – 12 June at Openlab's premises at Valhallavägen 79, accepted and assembled teams will come up with concepts for this. Over a few days we will use Design Thinking exercises to start from a user perspective in developing a concept. Through ideation, prototyping and testing, with support from Openlab's coaches, the teams will each develop a final concept. This final concept they will then program as a test version during the rest of the Hackathon. After the Commutathon, one or more concepts will be developed further and one could become something that is tested in Sveriges Radio's digital offering.**
