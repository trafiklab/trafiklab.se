Trafiklab loves hacks where you can create awesome stuff using open data, so of course we wanted to take part in Hack46 this spring.

The first hack takes place in Uppsala on the weekend of 28 - 29 March, followed by a hack in Stockholm on the weekend of 18-19 April. Naturally, we'll be there both weekends.

So what kind of hack is it? Well, at Hack46 we want you, together with other cheerful people, to get to use technology, build prototypes of new things, and in that way learn new things and get to know others. We want you to be inspired to use technology to solve problems and improve society.

**Are there prizes?**

A nice prize goes to the entry that comes up with the most innovative, unexpected way of using one or more APIs or technical tools. Collaboration between and within teams is highly valued!

**How do you prepare?**

Sign up, think of an idea and feel free to try out some of the APIs. But you don't need more than that; then all you have to do is show up on the first day of the hack with a computer and a good mood! For those of you who are missing team members there will be a mingle evening where we make sure everyone finds their teammates. A good team ideally has 5 members.

Interested? Sign up here: [https://hack46.com/](https://hack46.com/)

See you there!
