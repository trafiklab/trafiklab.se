date: 2015-12-16
draft: true

---

Version 1.1 of Trafikverket's open API is now being launched.

For the train announcements (TrainAnnouncement) a number of new fields are included, among them the technical train number that many have asked for. The big change is that the dataset is extended to also include arrivals and departures at non-advertised operational points.

For the road data it is primarily the addition of the safety-related message, SafetyRelatedMessage.

More information [here](http://api.trafikinfo.trafikverket.se/Content/ContentDetails/567177465794375858cb63f9).
